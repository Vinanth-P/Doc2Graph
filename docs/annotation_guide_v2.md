# Doc2Graph v2 Annotation Guidelines: Corporate Finance & Entity Relations

**Version:** 2.0  
**Scope:** Narrative English documents, corporate filings, press releases, M&A disclosures, and commercial news.  
**Audience:** Human annotators, domain experts, and technical reviewers.

---

## 1. Core Principles

1. **Evidence-Based Annotation Only**: Every annotated relation must be directly supported by minimal sufficient text evidence within the document. Do not import background real-world knowledge that is unstated in the text.
2. **Exact Spans & Immutability**: Entity mentions must match the exact character slice `[start, end)` of the document text (`text[start:end] == mention_text`). Never approximate boundaries.
3. **Explicit Negatives vs. Unknowns**:
   - `decision="positive"`: The relationship is stated or affirmed in the text.
   - `decision="explicit_negative"`: The annotator reviewed this ordered pair and relation and found no claim of that relation within the declared annotation scope. An explicit denial is instead a positive relation-claim annotation with `polarity="negated"`; it is not automatically a no-relation example.
   - `decision="unknown"`: The pair was unreviewed, outside annotation scope, or context is inconclusive. **Never mark an unreviewed pair as negative.**
4. **Machine Proposals Are Not Ground Truth**: Heuristic proposals generated by `scripts/propose_finance_annotations.py` serve only as drafts (`review_status="pending_human_review"`). They become valid gold annotations only after human review and sign-off.

---

## 2. Entity Mention Conventions

| Entity Type | Description & Boundary Rules | Examples | Counter-Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ORG` | Legal corporate entities, institutions, government bodies, non-profits, investment firms. Include legal suffixes (Inc., LLC, Ltd.) if part of the noun phrase. Exclude leading determiners ("the", "a") unless part of the legal trade name. | `Microsoft`, `Goldman Sachs & Co.`, `OpenAI Inc.`, `The New York Times Company` | `"the bank"` (generic), `"technology sector"` |
| `PERSON` | Full names, single surnames, or professional names of individuals. Exclude titles and honors (CEO, Dr., Mr.). | `Satya Nadella`, `Nadella`, `Patrick Collison` | `CEO Satya Nadella` (exclude "CEO") |
| `GPE` | Countries, cities, states, municipalities. | `San Francisco`, `California`, `United Kingdom` | `"Silicon Valley"` (region/concept) |
| `PRODUCT` | Distinct commercial software, hardware, services, or brands. | `GitHub`, `Bridge`, `Slack`, `ChatGPT` | `"artificial intelligence"` |
| `MONEY` | Currency and numeric transaction value expressions. | `$7.5 billion`, `EUR 500 million`, `$5B` | `"substantial consideration"` |
| `DATE` | Calendar dates, fiscal years, or specific quarters. | `October 2024`, `2014`, `Q3 2023` | `"recently"`, `"in the past"` |

## 3. Relation Definitions, Direction, and Type Constraints

All relations are **directed**. Ensure the subject and object adhere strictly to the role definitions:

### 3.1 ACQUIRED
- **Direction:** `acquirer (ORG) -> target (ORG)`
- **Definition:** Acquirer purchases, merges, or absorbs target.
- **Distinction:**
  - If transaction is announced but not closed, annotate `modality="planned"`, `lifecycle="announced"`.
  - If merger agreement signed but awaiting regulatory approval, annotate `modality="conditional"`, `lifecycle="signed"`.
  - If deal completed or closed, annotate `modality="asserted"`, `lifecycle="completed"`.
  - If acquisition is terminated or blocked, annotate `polarity="negated"`, `lifecycle="cancelled"`.
- **Negative Examples:** Minority investment or commercial partnership is NOT `ACQUIRED`.

### 3.2 INVESTED_IN
- **Direction:** `investor (ORG|PERSON) -> investee (ORG)`
- **Definition:** Capital commitment or equity investment into an entity.
- **Distinction:** Equity stake or funding round vs debt loan or advisory.
- **Attributes:** If announced or prospective, mark `modality="planned"`.

### 3.3 ADVISED
- **Direction:** `adviser (ORG|PERSON) -> client (ORG)`
- **Definition:** Investment bank, financial consultancy, or law firm counsel a client on an M&A deal, restructuring, or listing.
- **Distinction:** The counterparty in the deal is NOT the adviser's client unless dual representation is explicitly stated.

### 3.4 FINANCED
- **Direction:** `financier (ORG|PERSON) -> borrower (ORG)`, matching the current ontology. Borrower PERSON requires an explicit ontology revision and supporting annotations.
- **Definition:** Providing debt capital, credit lines, syndicated loans, or bond underwriting.
- **Distinction:** Debt financing vs equity investment (`INVESTED_IN`).

### 3.5 FOUNDED
- **Direction:** `founder (PERSON|ORG) -> organization (ORG)`
- **Definition:** Founder or co-founder established the organization.
- **Distinction:** Later-hired executives (even founding CEOs who did not establish the company) are `EMPLOYED_BY`, not `FOUNDED`.

### 3.6 EMPLOYED_BY
- **Direction:** `employee (PERSON) -> employer (ORG)`
- **Definition:** Individual holds an executive office, board directorship, or employment contract.
- **Distinction:** Board membership can coexist with `MEMBER_OF`.

### 3.7 MEMBER_OF
- **Direction:** `member (PERSON) -> organization (ORG)`
- **Definition:** Individual is a member of an advisory council, political party, or association.

### 3.8 OWNS
- **Direction:** `owner (ORG|PERSON) -> owned_entity (ORG|PRODUCT)`
- **Definition:** Full or partial equity holding, parent ownership, or intellectual property ownership.

### 3.9 SUBSIDIARY_OF
- **Direction:** `subsidiary (ORG) -> parent (ORG)`
- **Definition:** Operating entity is a corporate subsidiary or controlled operating division of a parent entity.

## 4. Assertion Attributes: Polarity, Modality, and Lifecycle

For every positive relation, annotators must assign the three assertion dimensions:

### Polarity
- `affirmed`: The statement affirms the relationship.
- `negated`: The text explicitly refutes or denies the relationship (e.g. *"Acme denied that it had acquired Beta."*).
- `unknown`: Polarity is indeterminate.

### Modality
- `asserted`: Stated as a fact or completed occurrence.
- `planned`: Stated as a prospective intention, proposal, or bid (e.g. *"plans to acquire"*).
- `conditional`: Stated as subject to conditions, regulatory approvals, or financing hurdles.
- `rumored`: Reported as unconfirmed rumor or press leak.
- `unknown`: Modality is indeterminate.

### Lifecycle
- `announced`: Transaction or partnership officially disclosed to public.
- `signed`: Formal definitive agreement executed.
- `completed`: Closed, finalized, or historical state.
- `cancelled`: Deal abandoned, terminated, or blocked.
- `unknown`: Lifecycle stage not specified.

## 5. Reviewer Workflow & Quality Acceptance

1. **Step 1 - Entity Verification**:
   - Inspect mentions: check bounds against `text[start:end]`. Correct any trailing/leading punctuation.
2. **Step 2 - Directed Relation Evaluation**:
   - Identify candidate pairs. Confirm direction matches `config/ontology_v2.yaml`.
3. **Step 3 - Evidence Selection**:
   - Highlight minimal sufficient text range `[evidence_start, evidence_end]` proving the claim.
4. **Step 4 - Negative Decision Logging**:
   - For contested or co-occurring entities that have NO relation (e.g. co-advisers, unrelated co-mentioned targets), explicitly record `decision="explicit_negative"`.
5. **Step 5 - Sign-off**:
   - Set document and completed annotation `review_status="reviewed"`, record actual reviewer identity, guidelines version and an ISO document review timestamp with timezone. Set `data_origin="human_reviewed_source"` only for genuinely reviewed source documents. Pending annotations remain masked even inside a reviewed document.
   - Run verification tests via `python scripts/import_reviewed_finance.py --input <file> --output <dir>`.

## 6. Export and holdout limitations

The finance exporter now uses `2.dataset.2`. Each candidate contains `assertions_by_relation` and `supervision_by_relation`; do not combine all relation statuses into a single pair-level target. Unknown fields remain masked. Original annotations are retained for inspection. Repeated annotations for the same ordered pair and relation require adjudication or a future event-scoped schema; they are rejected rather than silently overwritten.

Reviewer metadata is an attestation, not automated proof that a person reviewed a file. Draft documents default to `data_origin="unverified"` and `review_status="pending_human_review"`. Machine proposal output uses `machine_draft`. The proposal CLI currently reads UTF-8 text files, not PDF binaries; extract text separately and verify source offsets before annotation.

The bundled pilot is **test-fixture material**, not verified human gold. Use `--allow-fixtures` only to test the importer. Keep those outputs out of training, calibration and independent evaluation. The model must never learn the bundled examples merely because their illustrative reviewer fields look plausible.

Splits use deterministic 60/15/15/10 hash buckets; small datasets need not contain those exact percentages or any test examples. `locked_test.jsonl` is plain text, not a sealed vault: the manifest correctly records `locked_test_sealed=false` and `release_holdout_available=false`. A separate custodian/access-control process, verified fresh data and documented model freeze are required before claiming an untouched release holdout. Do not tune seeds to obtain a favorable partition.

Near-duplicate detection is a character-shingle heuristic, not a guarantee against all company/deal/template overlap. All records contribute grouping links before exclusion. Only identical text **and** equivalent annotation provenance are automatically excluded. Differently annotated copies stay in the same partition for review. Split assignments can change when the corpus/group graph changes; freeze a versioned manifest before training and audit subsequent imports against that version.
